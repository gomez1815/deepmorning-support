01. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Deep Morning, an independently developed productivity application operated by Taketo Horigome ("Deep Morning," "we," "our"), handles information when you use the Deep Morning mobile app, its connected features, and this support website.

Questions or requests about privacy can be sent to liverdom@icloud.com.

02. Information we handle

Account and profile

When you create an account, our authentication provider processes your email address, sign-in credentials, account identifiers, and authentication information. Deep Morning may store your display name, username, time zone, profile picture, biography, school or work information, hobbies, goals, profile visibility settings, and onboarding preferences that you choose to provide.

Focus, tasks, and planning

The app stores study subjects, plans, focus-session history, duration, pauses, ratings, interruption counts, and related productivity information primarily in an on-device database. Some features, including synchronized study tasks, task progress, calendar events created or imported into Deep Morning, optional friend boosts, and onboarding settings, also use our cloud backend.

Coach and recommendations

Deep Morning Coach can use the focus tasks, goals, schedule, and progress information available to the app to generate planning suggestions. The current coaching interface is intended to support productivity, rather than to advertise to you. Information you choose to send through a connected service may be processed to provide the requested feature.

Information you choose to share

If you use the community features, we process content you explicitly publish, such as focus summaries, captions, comments, reactions, follows, and profile information made visible according to your settings. Reports and blocking information are also used to help manage the community.

Technical information

Our service providers may receive technical information needed to operate their services, such as IP addresses, request timestamps, authentication events, and error or security logs. This website itself does not load advertising scripts or site analytics trackers.

03. Calendar connections and image imports

iPhone Calendar: With your permission, Deep Morning reads events from the calendars you select to display your schedule and suggest available focus time. The iPhone Calendar integration reads those events on your device; it does not copy the selected iPhone calendar feed into Supabase or modify your Apple Calendar events.

Google Calendar: If connected, the app uses Google-authorized, read-only calendar access to retrieve events from calendars you select. The short-lived access token and calendar selection are stored on your device. Google events are shown in your schedule rather than copied into Deep Morning's cloud database by this connection. We use information received from Google Calendar only to provide calendar and related focus-planning features, not for advertising or to train general-purpose AI models.

Canvas Calendar: If you connect a Canvas calendar feed, the app downloads the feed and sends calendar contents to our Supabase-backed synchronization service to create and update events in your account. New connections store the private feed address in secure storage on the device; connection metadata and imported events can be retained by our backend. Treat a Canvas feed URL as private.

Schedule screenshot import: When you choose an image for schedule recognition, Deep Morning sends the selected image over an authenticated connection to its schedule-reading/OCR service. The application code reads and analyzes the image in memory and returns extracted text and suggested calendar events for your review. It does not intentionally write or archive the original uploaded image in a permanent image store. Infrastructure may use temporary buffers and retain technical request or error logs; we have not verified every hosting-level backup or logging configuration. If you accept suggested events, those events may be stored in your Deep Morning calendar. Upload only screenshots you are comfortable submitting for processing.

Your choice: Calendar access and image import are optional. You can disconnect calendars inside the app and revoke iPhone permissions in iOS Settings. Disconnecting a source may not automatically delete events that were previously imported into your Deep Morning calendar.

04. Focus Protection and Screen Time

On supported Apple devices, Focus Protection uses Apple's Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Device Activity technologies to help restrict selected apps and websites during focus sessions. You choose whether to authorize these features and which apps, categories, or websites to protect.

Apple's authorization and protected-app selections are handled through iOS and app/extension storage on your device. Deep Morning can record local session-related events, such as focus exits and shield interactions, to calculate your focus statistics. We do not use protected-app selections to serve targeted advertisements.

The current app does not provide a general report of all of your device's Screen Time or detailed app-by-app website usage history. You can change or revoke Screen Time permissions in your iPhone settings.

05. Community visibility

Deep Morning includes optional discovery, following, sharing, comments, and friend-boost features. If you make your profile discoverable, other signed-in members may see your display name, username, and profile fields permitted by your visibility settings. Your public profile photo is served through a publicly readable image URL and may be accessible to anyone who has that link.

Focus-session details are not automatically posted to the Feed. When you explicitly share a session, the associated details become visible to the audience allowed by the community's access rules. Other users may copy or capture information that you choose to share.

Optional friend-boost comparisons can use daily focus-minute totals stored in our backend when you opt in. The feature is designed to share encouragement and limited comparison information rather than exposing another person's complete session history.

06. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and save your chosen profile settings.
  • To run focus sessions, provide recommendations, display your calendar, and calculate progress.
  • To process optional calendar connections and schedule images that you submit.
  • To support voluntary social sharing, friend connections, boosts, reporting, and moderation.
  • To answer support requests, troubleshoot failures, prevent misuse, and protect the service.
  • To meet applicable legal obligations and maintain the safety and integrity of Deep Morning.

We do not sell your personal information. The current app does not integrate third-party behavioral advertising SDKs, and Deep Morning does not use your calendar data or Focus Protection selections for targeted advertising.

07. Service providers and integrations

We use third-party services only when needed to provide features you use or to operate the service. These may include:

  • Supabase: authentication, cloud database, profile image storage, calendar synchronization, and server-side functions. Privacy information.
  • Apple: device calendars, notifications, and Family Controls/Screen Time permissions managed by iOS. Privacy information.

We expect providers handling information on our behalf to apply appropriate contractual, technical, and organizational protections consistent with applicable law. Their independent services may also be subject to their own privacy policies.

08. Storage and retention

On-device information: Your local session records, preferences, and securely stored connection credentials remain on your device until you remove them, reset the app, disconnect an integration, or uninstall Deep Morning, subject to iOS backup and device behavior.

Cloud information: Account records, profile information, shared community data, synced tasks, imported calendars, and related settings may remain in our cloud systems while your account is active or as needed to provide a requested service. User-generated content may remain visible until you remove it or it is removed through moderation.

Account deletion: You can permanently delete your account inside Deep Morning under Settings → Delete Your Account. The authenticated deletion process removes the account from Supabase Authentication and the related database records linked to the account through cascading deletion, including your profile, community posts and comments, cloud tasks, and stored calendar connections and events. It also removes profile photos held in the user-owned folder in our profile image storage. The app then attempts to remove locally stored focus and planning records, scheduled reminders, and protected-app selections on the device.

Backups, logs and exceptions: Cloud service providers and hosting systems may temporarily retain backup copies, security logs, or other records under their own operating schedules. Certain information may be retained where needed for legal compliance, fraud prevention, security, or dispute resolution. Content previously copied or independently saved by other users cannot always be recalled. If device cleanup fails after cloud deletion, the app will inform you and you may need to reinstall it. We do not promise immediate deletion from every backup or infrastructure log.

09. Your privacy choices

  • Profile: update your display information and choose whether your profile appears in Discover; control the visibility of supported profile fields.
  • Community: choose what to publish, delete your posts where available, unfollow others, and use blocking and reporting features.
  • Calendars: disconnect Google, Canvas, or iPhone Calendar in Deep Morning; revoke iPhone Calendar permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Focus Protection: adjust protected apps or revoke Family Controls authorization through the device's settings.
  • Notifications: manage local notification permissions through Deep Morning or iOS Settings.
  • Delete your account: open Settings → Delete Your Account and complete the confirmation steps. You do not need to email support to use the in-app deletion flow.
  • Data requests: for questions, requests to access or correct personal data, or help if deletion fails, email liverdom@icloud.com. Do not send passwords or authentication codes.

We will review requests in accordance with applicable law and may ask you to confirm account ownership. Some rights depend on your jurisdiction.

10. How to delete your account

Deep Morning provides a direct, permanent account-deletion option to signed-in users:

  1. Open Deep Morning → Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete Your Account.
  3. Read the deletion explanation, type DELETE, and confirm the permanent deletion.

The request is authenticated using your signed-in account. When successful, the backend deletes the account and associated cloud records as described above, and the app signs you out after attempting on-device cleanup. You cannot restore a deleted account or its focus history through the app.

If the feature does not complete or you cannot access your account, email liverdom@icloud.com for assistance. We may need to verify your account ownership before processing a support request. We will handle requests as required by applicable law.

11. Security

Some information you intentionally publish, including publicly accessible profile images, is not private. No internet service or device storage system can guarantee absolute security.

For security reports or suspected unauthorized access, contact liverdom@icloud.com.
