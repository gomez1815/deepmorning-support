DEEP MORNING GUIDE

Community

Learn about the social side of Deep Morning and where to report concerns.

Community features are intended to make focus feel encouraging and shared. Some features may vary by app version or account.

Getting started

  1. Find the community area

    Open the Community or Feed area, if it is available in your version of Deep Morning.

  2. Explore thoughtfully

    Review available posts, progress updates, or other community views without feeling pressure to compare your focus with anyone else.

  3. Keep shared content safe

    Avoid publishing personal contact details, private calendar information, passwords, or sensitive information.

  4. Report a concern

    Use an in-app report control if one is available, or contact support with enough context to identify the issue safely.

Common questions

I cannot see a community feature. Why?

Community options can depend on your app version and what is currently available. Check for updates and contact support if you think something is missing.

How do I report inappropriate content?

Use the report option on the relevant content when available. Otherwise, email support with a brief description and non-sensitive context.

Are community results or rankings the same for everyone?

Displayed community views can vary. If a result looks incorrect, share the screen name or view and approximate date with support.

Still need a hand?

We'd be happy to help with your question or a problem you're experiencing.
