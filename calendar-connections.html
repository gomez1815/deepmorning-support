Calendar connections

Bring your existing schedule into the place where you plan your focus.

Your calendar helps Deep Morning make better use of the time you have. Connection options and available permissions can differ depending on your device and app version.

Getting started

  1. Open calendar settings

    In Deep Morning, open Settings and look for Calendar Connections, or visit the connection flow from Schedule.

  2. Choose a calendar source

    Select an available option such as Apple Calendar or Google Calendar. You may also see an option to import an .ics calendar file.

  3. Approve access

    Follow the on-screen sign-in or iOS permission prompts. Only grant the access you want Deep Morning to use.

  4. Review your schedule

    Return to Schedule, confirm the events appear on the correct dates, and check your device time zone if times look wrong.

Common questions

Why are my calendar events missing?

Check calendar permissions in iOS Settings and make sure the correct calendar account or source is selected. Then reopen Schedule.

My event times look wrong. What should I check?

Check your device time zone and the time zone of the original calendar event or imported file.

How do I reconnect a calendar?

Go back to Calendar Connections and follow the available reconnect or authorization flow. You can also review access in your device or calendar account settings.
