Focus Protection

Get help with the iOS permissions used for a more intentional session.

Focus Protection is designed to help reduce distractions during a session. Its behavior depends on your choices, iOS permissions, and device support.

Getting started

  1. Find Focus Protection

    Open Deep Morning Settings and locate the Focus Protection or related Screen Time controls.

  2. Review the permission request

    If iOS asks for Screen Time or Family Controls authorization, read the system prompt before choosing whether to allow it.

  3. Configure your focus

    Follow any available app-selection or protection options in the app. The exact setup depends on your iOS version.

  4. Start a session

    Begin a focus session and check that the protection you selected behaves as expected.

Common questions

Why is Focus Protection not activating?

Review Screen Time authorization and any app-selection settings. Then restart Deep Morning and try a short test session.

Does Deep Morning read my other apps?

iOS controls access to Screen Time-related capabilities. For details on what Deep Morning collects or processes, see the Privacy Policy.

Can I turn off authorization?

You can review and change app permissions in iOS Settings. Available in-app controls may depend on the version you are using.
