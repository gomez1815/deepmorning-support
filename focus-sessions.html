DEEP MORNING GUIDE

Focus sessions

Everything you need to start, complete, and reflect on a focused session.

Deep Morning helps you make space for one meaningful task at a time. Use this guide to set up a focus session and understand what happens after it ends.

Getting started

  1. Choose what to focus on

    Open Today, pick an activity or task, or use a recommendation if one is available.

  2. Set up your session

    Choose a suitable duration and start your focus timer. Review any options before you begin.

  3. Stay with your task

    Keep your focus session running. If you enabled Focus Protection, iOS may restrict selected apps during the session.

  4. Finish and reflect

    When the session ends, give it a rating when prompted. Review your completed activity in your progress or stats views.

Common questions

I cannot start a focus session. What should I try?

Close and reopen Deep Morning, confirm you have selected a valid duration, and check for an app update. If the issue continues, tell us what happens after you tap Start.

What if a session does not appear in my progress?

Check that the session was completed and that you are viewing the expected day. If it is still missing, email support with the date and approximate time.

Can I change my task after starting?

Available controls depend on the current app version. If you do not see an edit option, complete your session and choose a different task next time.
