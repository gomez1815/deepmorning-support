Account & profile

Keep your profile up to date and get help with account access.

Your account and profile are where you manage how Deep Morning recognizes you. You can also find privacy information and account-related help here.

Getting started

  1. Open your profile

    In Deep Morning, visit Profile or Settings to find your available account and personal-preference controls.

  2. Update your details

    Edit supported fields such as your display name, profile photo, goals, or interests, then save your changes.

  3. Review account options

    Use the available sign-in, sign-out, and account management actions for your current version.

  4. Request help if needed

    If you cannot access your account or find an expected option, contact support. Never email passwords or verification codes.

Common questions

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings and find the account deletion option in Deep Morning. Follow the on-screen confirmation. If you cannot access it, contact support for assistance.

My profile changes are not showing. What should I do?

Check your connection, save your edits again, and reopen your profile. If the issue remains, tell support which field did not update.

Where can I read about my data?

Read the Deep Morning Privacy Policy linked below for information about data handling and your privacy options.
